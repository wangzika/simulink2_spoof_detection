# Cover Letter Draft for GPS Solutions

Dear Editor-in-Chief and Associate Editors,

Please consider our manuscript, "Environment-Adaptive Sequential GLRT for GNSS Spoofing Detection Using Raw Observations and LiDAR-Inertial Consistency," for publication in GPS Solutions.

This manuscript addresses a practical limitation of measurement-level GNSS spoofing detectors: fixed-threshold RAIM, innovation, and residual monitors often confuse spoofing with benign urban degradation caused by weak signals, poor geometry, RTK ambiguity changes, and LiDAR-inertial alignment transients. We propose an environment-adaptive sequential GLRT that combines raw pseudorange residuals, receiver pseudorange diagnostics, and LiDAR-GNSS consistency, while adapting the evidence threshold with C/N0, DOP, satellite count, RTK ratio, and odometry quality.

The manuscript contributes:

1. A reproducible raw-observation and LiDAR-inertial GNSS spoofing detection platform with standalone RTKLIB post-processing, RINEX feature extraction, broadcast-ephemeris pseudorange residuals, RAIM statistics, and observation-level pseudorange attack injection.
2. An environment-adaptive sequential GLRT detector that uses multi-cue consistency gating and CUSUM-style confirmation to reduce false alarms under degraded non-attack conditions.
3. A controlled experiment matrix covering clean data, degraded non-attack data, attack strength, ramp duration, attack type, baseline comparisons, ablations, parameter sensitivity, and temporal held-out validation.

The current draft is positioned as a reproducible method and platform paper for receiver-output and post-processing GNSS spoofing detection. Before formal submission, the authors will finalize the Word-format manuscript, confirm data availability language, and add any additional independent route or replay-spoofing evidence available at submission time.

The manuscript is original, is not under consideration elsewhere, and all authors have approved its submission.

Sincerely,

Zhibo Wang
